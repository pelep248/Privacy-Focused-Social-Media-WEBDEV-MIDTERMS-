About us

AI is everywhere now, and a lot of apps collect more of our data than we realize. We made Hush because we think people should be able to talk and share ideas without being watched.

Hush is a prototype built for a Web Development midterm. It uses only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

Our goals

  • Keep user data private
  • Keep it simple and lightweight
  • Let people express themselves freely

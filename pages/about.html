About us

AI is everywhere now, and a lot of apps collect more of our data than we realize. I made Hush because I think people should be able to talk and share ideas without being watched.

Hush is a prototype built for a DS315 - Web Development, MIDTERMS EXAM. It uses only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

Goals

  • Keep user data private
  • Keep it simple and lightweight
  • Let people express themselves freely

Our privacy promise

  • No server. Your account and posts are saved only in your own browser (localStorage).
  • No tracking. No cookies for ads, no analytics, no outside scripts.
  • No email or phone number needed to sign up.
  • You can delete everything in Settings at any time.
  • Anonymous posts hide your username from other people.

Note: because this is only a school prototype, passwords are stored as plain text in localStorage. A real app would need a backend and hashed passwords.

Hush: a school project. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
